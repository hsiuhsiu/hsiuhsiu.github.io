Yi-Hsiu Chen

I am an applied cryptographer at Coinbase. Previously, I was a research scientist in the Ads Core ML team at Facebook.

I completed my Ph.D. in theoretical cryptography at Harvard University, advised by Salil Vadhan. Before that, I studied physics at National Taiwan University and computer science at Columbia University.

Email: yihsiu@alumni.harvard.edu · LinkedIn · GitHub

Projects

  • cb-mpc — Coinbase’s open-source library for secure multiparty computation and threshold signing, with protocol specifications and theory.
  • Cue — a small, fast, open-source macOS launcher I built for everyday use. Fork it and make it your own.

Research

My work spans applied and theoretical cryptography, including zero-knowledge proofs, secret sharing, pseudorandomness, and differential privacy.

During a visit to Academia Sinica in Taiwan in 2015–2016, I was hosted by Kai-Min Chung, who introduced me to quantum information.
